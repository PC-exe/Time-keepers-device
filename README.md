**A Timekeeper’s Radio Device in a speculative world:**
Timekeepers keep track of people’s perceptual time and 
also have the ability of controlling it, as a method of
infringement in this dystopian world ..
They percieve it as music, which is used as a language
to understand other people’s perceptual time..
Music exists as not only an indicator of how time
passes for a human being, it’s also a source of
personal joy for the timekeeper, a form of
entertainment..
As they toy with humanity’s perceptive realities, music
amplifies the satisfaction of their dominion..
